# Clawd animations

Clawd 400 by 风花叶 — CC BY 4.0 — https://github.com/khiqwq/clawd-400

The 400 files `001.png` to `400.png` are the Clawd 400 pixel animations, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: ClaudeWeb redrew each original 480×480 GIF as a horizontal strip of its 40×40 frames
(the GIFs are those drawings scaled 12×). Frame timings are kept in
`app/src/modules/clawd/data/clawd-catalog.json`. The drawings themselves are unchanged.
The conversion is `app/scripts/build-clawd-assets.py`.

Clawd 400 is unofficial fan art. The Clawd character and the Claude marks belong to
Anthropic; the licence grants no rights to them.
